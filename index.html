About

Simon Vudiya Lirova is an agricultural scientist with a BSc in Horticulture and an MSc in Plant Pathology from the University of Nairobi. He has experience in plant health diagnostics, pest and disease management, agricultural research, and data collection and analysis. He currently serves as a Program Officer at the Kenya National Academy of Sciences (KNAS), contributing to initiatives that promote scientific research, science communication, and evidence-based policymaking. He is also developing skills in GIS mapping, R programming, and digital technologies to advance data-driven agriculture and innovation. His interests include plant virology, agricultural data science, and the application of technology to address agricultural challenges in Kenya and across Africa.
